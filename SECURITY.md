# Security

## Reporting a problem

Please report security problems privately, not in a public issue:
**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/Sangam1112/Arrow-Browser/security/advisories/new)** (GitHub's private reporting; only you and the maintainer can see it).

Helpful to include:
- what an attacker could do, and what they need (a page you visit, a file you download, access to your computer…)
- steps to reproduce, or a small test page
- the Arrow version (Settings → About) and your Linux distribution

You'll get a reply once the report has been looked at. Fixes ship as a signed update, and the report is published after most people have updated.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release gets fixes. Arrow updates itself, or use **Settings → About → Check for updates**.

## What belongs here

- Arrow's own code: the address bar, the padlock and certificate checks, downloads, saved passwords, the updater and its signature check, Arrow's own pages, and its privacy protections.
- Problems in the web engine itself come from your Linux distribution's packages: report those to your distribution, and keep your system up to date to get their fixes.
